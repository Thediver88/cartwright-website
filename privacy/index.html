Who is responsible

What this website collects

Nothing, beyond what any web server needs to deliver a page. The site has no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no analytics, and the font is stored on this site rather than loaded from another service.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). To deliver pages and keep the service secure, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The plugins (Cut & Pack and Sample Shelf)

The plugins run on your own website. They send no data to Cartwright or anyone else.

Your rights
