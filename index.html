Built for real shops

Whole boxes, always

Rounds up so customers get at least what they need. An exact fit is never rounded up, and stock is counted in boxes.

Prices you can trust

Your shop recalculates every price itself. Nobody can change it in their browser to pay less.

Any checkout

Works with WooCommerce's new block checkout and the classic one, on desktop and mobile.

Min, max and steps

Set the smallest and largest order, and cutting steps like 10 cm, with clear messages for customers.

No tracking

No external services and no data collected. It runs entirely on your own website.

Lightweight

Loads only on products that use it, so the rest of your shop stays as fast as it is today.
