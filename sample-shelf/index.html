Sample Shelf

A free WooCommerce plugin for flooring, tile and fabric shops. Shoppers ask for a sample of a product before they commit to the full order.

Coming soon to WordPress.org. It is with the WordPress.org review team now. This page will link to it as soon as it is approved.

What it does

  • Adds a “Request a sample” button next to Add to cart, on the products you choose.
  • Samples are free by default, or you can set a small price.
  • Limits samples per order (five by default), so a cart cannot fill up with them.
  • Labels the sample clearly in the cart, the checkout, the emails and the order.
  • Leaves your stock alone by default, so samples do not use up what you sell.
  • Works with simple and variable products, and with the block and classic cart and checkout.

Safe by design

Your shop decides the price, the limit and whether a product is eligible. Nothing a shopper changes in their browser can alter them. Sample Shelf collects no data and talks to no outside services.

Works with Cut & Pack

Selling tile or flooring by the square metre? With Cut & Pack 1.2 or newer, shoppers can order a sample without entering a room size, and the full order still works as before.
